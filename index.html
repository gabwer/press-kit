01Profile

The art of shaping perception

For over twenty years, Gabriel Werlen has pushed the boundaries of mentalism — combining scientific rigour with artistic creativity to craft pure illusions that come to life directly in the spectator's mind, by subtly playing with their perceptions.

An engineer by training (CentraleSupélec) and a former corporate executive, he is today recognised as one of the foremost creators of propless, impromptu method. Author of six books — including The Green Neck System and Iceberg — his work has captivated peers such as Derren Brown, Michael Weber and Michael Murray.
